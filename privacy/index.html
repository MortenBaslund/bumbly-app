Dansk

Kort fortalt

Bumbly indsamler ingen oplysninger om dig. Vi har ingen servere, ingen reklamer og ingen sporing. Alt, hvad du lægger i Bumbly, ligger på din telefon, i Apples Kalender og Påmindelser og i jeres egen iCloud.

Hvor dine ting ligger

Begivenheder og opgaver gemmes i Apples Kalender og Påmindelser. Familiens fælles ting (fx ønsker, madplan, point, præmier og billeder) gemmes i iCloud hos den administrator, der har oprettet hjemmet, og deles kun med dem, der er inviteret til hjemmet. Billeder slettes automatisk: på beskeder dagen efter, på begivenheder en uge efter og af udført arbejde, når det er godkendt (senest efter 14 dage). Beskeder slettes efter 7 dage. Lydbeskeder bliver på telefonen.

For at familiens telefoner kan give besked, når noget ændres, lægger Bumbly en kort note i jeres iCloud om, hvad der skete (fx at en aftale blev oprettet eller slettet, eller at nogens point blev rettet). Noterne deles kun i hjemmet og slettes efter 2 døgn.

Din konto

Bumbly har ingen egen konto og intet login. Du genkendes på den iCloud, telefonen er logget ind med. Bumbly læser kun dit fornavn fra iCloud for at foreslå dit navn – aldrig din e-mail eller adgangskode.

Tale og Bumbly AI

Lydbeskeder skrives om til tekst af Apple på telefonen, når telefonen kan det. Bumbly AI bruger Apples model på telefonen – dine spørgsmål sendes ikke til os eller andre.

Køb

Abonnementer købes og administreres af Apple. Vi ser ikke dine betalingsoplysninger.

Slet dine data

I appen under Indstillinger → Slet min konto sletter du alt om dig i Bumbly. Ligger hjemmet i din iCloud, slettes det for hele familien. Apples Kalender og Påmindelser røres ikke – dem styrer du i Apples apps.

Børn

Børn kan have en profil, som deres voksne styrer. Vi indsamler heller ikke noget om børn.

English

In short

Bumbly collects no information about you. We have no servers, no ads and no tracking. Everything you put in Bumbly lives on your phone, in Apple's Calendar and Reminders, and in your own iCloud.

Where your things are stored

Events and tasks are stored in Apple's Calendar and Reminders. The family's shared things (e.g. wishes, meal plan, points, prizes and photos) are stored in the iCloud of the administrator who created the home, and are shared only with people invited to the home. Photos are deleted automatically: on messages the next day, on events a week later, and of completed work once approved (at the latest after 14 days). Messages are deleted after 7 days. Voice messages stay on the phone.

So that the family's phones can notify each other when something changes, Bumbly puts a short note in your iCloud about what happened (e.g. that an event was created or deleted, or that someone's points were adjusted). The notes are shared only within the home and are deleted after 2 days.

Your account

Bumbly has no account of its own and no sign-in. You're recognised by the iCloud account the phone is signed in to. Bumbly only reads your first name from iCloud to suggest your name – never your email or password.

Speech and Bumbly AI

Voice messages are turned into text by Apple on the phone when the phone supports it. Bumbly AI uses Apple's on-device model – your questions are not sent to us or anyone else.

Purchases

Subscriptions are purchased and managed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Delete your data

In the app under Settings → Delete my account you delete everything about you in Bumbly. If the home is stored in your iCloud, it is deleted for the whole family. Apple's Calendar and Reminders are not touched – you manage those in Apple's apps.

Children

Children can have a profile managed by their adults. We collect nothing about children either.
